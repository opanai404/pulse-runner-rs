# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Participants are expected to keep discussions respectful, direct, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Give technical feedback with clear reasoning and actionable suggestions.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them promptly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants when behavior is harmful to the project community.
